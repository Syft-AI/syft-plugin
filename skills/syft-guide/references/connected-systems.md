# Connected systems

Syft is stronger when blended with the user's own data. Any tool that brings first-party or third-party account data into the host can be combined with priority accounts to decide who to work and what to say. The list below is the common set, not a limit.

| Category | Examples | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| CRM | HubSpot, Salesforce | Customer and opportunity state; where to create and tag accounts |
| Sequencer | Apollo, lemlist, Outreach, Salesloft | Where priority accounts and contacts get enrolled; custom variables (if supported) carry the value proposition into the message |
| Call recordings | Gong, Fathom, Granola | What prospects said; pairs with evidence for revive and follow-up plays |
| Collaboration | Slack, email | Where to post alerts and weekly lists |
| ABM and intent | 6sense, Bombora, RB2B, Vector | A fuller view of the account to sharpen the value proposition or priority |
| Social proof and reviews | G2, TrustRadius, Trustpilot | Competitive context for the value proposition |
| Contact providers | ZoomInfo, Apollo | Contacts and emails before spending Syft credits |

## When combining

- Syft does not know CRM state. Offer to filter customers and open opportunities through the CRM; the user decides.
- Suggest a connected contact provider before `enrich_contacts` when one is available.
- Recommend tagging records created from Syft data "syft" so results can be attributed. Ask before tagging.
- When another source disagrees with Syft's evidence, show both and say where they differ.
